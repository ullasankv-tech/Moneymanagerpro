Money Manager Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Developer: Ullasan KV, Poyanad, Kannur, Kerala, India

1. What we collect

Registration details you enter: name, phone number, email address, city, username and avatar. These are stored on your device and sent to our Firebase (Google Cloud) database so we can understand our user base and improve the app. The financial data you enter (transactions, bills, loans, goals, investments, notes and bill photos) is stored on your device, encrypted with AES-256.

2. Cloud backup (optional)

If you use Cloud Backup, a copy of your app data is uploaded to our Firebase database under your username so you can restore it on another phone. You can delete it at any time (see section 7). Bill photos are never uploaded.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone — voice typing only; speech is converted to text by your phone's speech service.
  • Notifications and alarms — daily, bill and EMI reminders.
  • Camera / photos — only when you scan a bill. The photo stays on your device; the text is read on your device (a reading engine is downloaded the first time you scan).
  • Fingerprint — unlock is handled by Android; we never receive your biometric data.

4. Purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; we only learn that the purchase was completed.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. Service providers: Google Firebase (storage) and Google Play (billing). This version of the app shows no ads.

6. Security

Data on your device is encrypted. Cloud backups are stored in our Firebase database. No method of storage is 100% secure.

7. Your choices and deletion

In the app: Settings → Delete My Account & Data removes your profile and cloud backup from our server and erases all data on your device. Without the app, see how to request deletion. Settings → Clear All Data erases only your financial entries.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
