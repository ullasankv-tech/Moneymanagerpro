1. Getting started

  1. Install Money Manager Pro from Google Play (or your APK) and open it.
  2. Register once with your name, phone, email, city, a username and a password. You then log in with your username.
  3. Lock the app (optional): open More → Security to set or change your PIN, and turn on fingerprint unlock if your phone supports it.
  4. Choose your currency: More → Currency. You can add your own currency to the table.
On the Android app, your data is stored in the app’s private files on the phone (not in Chrome). Entries are encrypted. JSON backups are files you save or share (for example to Google Drive).
The app is designed for one user per phone. Use More → About to see the app version and build number (same as the Play / APK build).

3. Home: adding transactions

Smart Quick-Add (fastest)

Type or speak a short sentence, then tap Go. Examples: Petrol 250 yesterday, Salary 45000 on 1st. The app finds the amount, date and category. Check the preview and confirm.

To use your voice, tap the microphone and allow the microphone permission.

Scan a bill

Tap the scan bill icon, take a photo or pick one from your gallery. The app reads the amount, date and shop and suggests a category. Correct anything that looks wrong, then Save Expense. A 🧾 icon appears on that transaction so you can view the bill photo later.

The first scan needs the internet once, to download the reading engine. Bill photos stay on your phone and are not included in backups.

Add Transaction (manual)

Choose Income or Expense, enter the amount, pick a category, set the date and an optional note, then save. Tap any transaction in Recent Transactions to edit or delete it.

Other things on Home

  • Today's Spending / Set Limit — set a daily spending limit and see a warning when you go past it.
  • Recurring Due Today — recurring items (rent, subscriptions) that are due appear here to confirm.
  • Notes — a note for each date.

Categories

More → Category Master lets you add your own categories or reset to the defaults.

4. Bills & EMIs

Bill Reminders (More → Bill Reminders)

  • Tap + Add Bill: name, amount, due day of the month and category.
  • Tick Phone alarm message to get a notification on the due date even when the app is closed. Pick the alarm time and how many days before.
  • When you pay, tap ✅ Pay. Only then is an expense recorded. If you tap Unpaid, that expense is removed again.

EMI / Loan Tracker (More → Savings Goals area, or Goals tab → EMI / Loan Tracker)

Use this to track a bank-style loan with a fixed monthly EMI (including long tenures such as 120 months).

  • + Add EMI / Loan — enter loan name, total principal, monthly EMI, number of months, start date and interest rate (% per year).
  • Pay EMI — records a matching expense under Bills and advances the paid count.
  • ↩ Undo — cancels the last payment and removes that expense from Income & Expense.
  • 📊 Schedule — builds a full month-by-month amortization table (opening balance, EMI, principal, interest, closing balance) and opens the Android share sheet so you can open or save it in Excel (or Google Sheets / Drive) if installed.
  • 🗑️ Delete — removes the loan from the tracker only if no payments are linked. If you have already paid EMIs, a message box explains that you must use ↩ Undo (or delete those expenses on Home) first, so orphan entries do not stay in Income & Expense.
On the cardMeaning
Monthly EMIYour instalment amount
PaidCash already paid (linked expenses)
RemainingOutstanding principal only (same idea as a bank loan balance)
Remaining follows banking practice: it is the principal still left after EMIs you have marked paid — not “principal + all future interest”. A short line under the card may show how much you would still pay if you continued every EMI to the end (principal left + future interest).
Interest is calculated on a reducing-balance basis. If your EMI is higher than the pure formula amount, the loan may finish in fewer months than the tenure you entered; the schedule and Remaining figures follow the actual balance.

Subscriptions

Register → Subscriptions tracks recurring charges and suggests ones you may want to cancel.

5. Budgets & goals

  • Budget Planner (More → Budget Planner): set a monthly limit for each category. You get a notice at 80% of a limit and a confirmation if an expense would go over it.
  • Savings Goals (More → Savings Goals): add a goal with a target amount, add money to it and watch the progress. The SIP Calculator tells you the monthly amount needed to reach a goal.
  • Goal Boost (Register): move surplus cash into your goals with one tap.

6. Pockets & Safe to Spend

Smart Money Pockets (Register → Pockets)

Pockets are virtual envelopes (Emergency, Goals, Fun, Travel…). Moving money into a pocket does not create an expense; it only records that you have set it aside.

Auto-Split Rules

  1. Enter the % for Emergency, Goals and any other pocket (the total cannot be over 100%).
  2. Tap Run Auto-Split on Remaining Balance.

The percentages are applied to your remaining balance: this month's income − this month's expenses − money you have already set aside. The card shows this calculation before you run it. Each income can be split only once, and Undo last auto-split reverses it.

Spending money you set aside

If an expense is bigger than your free cash, the app warns you. If you continue, the shortfall is taken out of your pockets (other pockets first, then Goals, and Emergency last). If you later delete or reduce that expense, the money goes back.

Safe to Spend (Register → Safe-to-Spend)

Shows how much you can spend per day for the rest of the month:

(Cash remaining − Upcoming unpaid bills − Goal buffer − Emergency buffer) ÷ Days left

  • Cash remaining = this month's income − expenses − money set aside
  • Upcoming bills = unpaid Bill Reminders due this month
  • Goal buffer = 5% of the amount your goals still need
  • Emergency buffer and days left can be changed under Adjust Assumptions

Salary Day Automation

In More → Salary Day Automation choose your salary date. On that day a Payday Checklist guides you: record the income, run the auto-split, review bills and check Safe to Spend.

Other Register tools

  • Sheets — a daily register view of your entries.
  • AI Insights — tips generated from your own data. They work offline and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Envelope carry-forward — choose what happens to unused pocket money at month end.

7. Investments & Trading

Money Manager Pro is a record-keeping tool. It does not place trades, give investment advice or connect to your broker. Rates and calculators are indicative only.
  • Invest tab: record purchase and sale entries for shares and other investments, with charges.
  • Trading tab: Capital Account (money moved in and out of trading), purchase/sale entries, Trading Performance, an Averaging calculator (new average price after buying more) and a Position Size calculator (how many shares for your risk).
  • Charges: edit the brokerage and tax rates to match your broker; they auto-fill new entries.
  • Live Market Prices (optional): enter the address of your own price service. Only share symbols are sent, never your quantities or amounts. Leave it empty to turn it off.

Income Tax & Capital Gains

Choose your tax regime, add your 80C entries and see capital-gains figures from your Invest entries. Use it as a guide, not as tax advice.

Cross-check with a bank statement

Upload your bank statement as an Excel file (columns: Date, Particulars, Debit, Credit, Balance) to compare it with your entries. Password-protected files work. The file is read on your phone only; nothing is uploaded or saved.

9. Reminders & alarms

  • Daily Reminder (More → Daily Reminder): pick a time and the app reminds you to enter that day's transactions, even when it is closed.
  • Bill alarms work the same way for each bill that has the alarm option turned on.
To make alarms reliable on your phone:
• Turn Auto Start ON for Money Manager Pro (Settings → Apps → Money Manager Pro → Auto start)
• Set Battery usage to Unrestricted
• Allow Notifications and Alarms & reminders when asked
Some phone makers (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, OnePlus) stop apps in the background unless you do this.

10. Backup, restore & deleting data

Open More → Export & Backup.

ButtonWhat it does
Backup now / Local Backup – JSONSaves a backup file you can keep or share (Drive, Files, email, WhatsApp)
Import JSON BackupRestores from a backup file you choose
Automatic Backup to JSON on loginOptional: creates a local JSON backup when you sign in
Clear All DataErases financial entries on this phone (account login may remain)
Delete My Account & DataRemoves your registration profile from our server (when online) and erases everything on this phone, then returns you to Login / Register. Type DELETE in the confirmation box. JSON files you saved elsewhere are not deleted.
There is no built-in cloud backup of your financial data. After Local Backup – JSON, save or share the file to Google Drive, Files or email — that is your cloud copy under your control.
New phone? On the old phone use Local Backup – JSON and save the file to Google Drive. Install the app on the new phone, register, then Import JSON Backup and pick that file. Bill photos are not part of backups.

The Activity log (More) shows your last 50 important actions such as deletes, auto-splits, undo and backups. It stays on your phone.

11. Premium

Premium is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play. It supports the app, gives you a PRO badge and unlocks premium-only features as they are added. Tap More → Go Premium. If you reinstall or change phones, open the same screen and tap Restore purchases (use the same Google account).

12. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to try
Reminder or alarm did not ringCheck Auto Start, Battery Unrestricted, Notifications and Alarms & reminders permissions (see section 9). Re-save the reminder after changing them.
Voice typing does nothingAllow the microphone permission and check your phone has a speech service installed.
Bill scan cannot read the billUse good light and keep the bill flat. Connect to the internet for the first scan. You can always type the details.
Premium not showing after purchaseTap Restore purchases and check you are on the same Google account.
Numbers look wrong after restoringRestore the same backup file again. Backups from before a feature existed will not contain data for that feature.
Import backup does nothingPick a JSON file created by this app’s Local Backup. A damaged or wrong file will not load.
Cannot delete an EMI / loanUse ↩ Undo for each paid EMI (or delete those expenses on Home), then delete the loan. A message box explains this if payments are still linked.
EMI Schedule does not open in ExcelIn the share sheet choose Excel, Sheets or Drive. Install Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets if needed, then try 📊 Schedule again.

Your app version and build are shown at More → About and on the login screen when the app is ready. Please include them when you ask for help. You can reach the developer through GitHub.

13. Privacy

Your financial entries are stored encrypted in the app’s private storage on your phone. You control any copy you export as JSON. Registration details may be stored so we can improve the app; financial cloud backup is not built in. The full policy is at Privacy Policy, and you can request deletion without the app at Delete account.
