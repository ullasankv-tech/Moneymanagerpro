Delete your Money Manager Pro account and data

Fastest way (in the app): open Settings → Delete My Account & Data, confirm, and type DELETE. This removes your profile and cloud backup from our server and erases everything stored on your phone.

What is deleted

  • Your registration profile (name, phone, email, city, username, avatar) on our Firebase database
  • Your cloud backup (if you ever created one)
  • All data stored in the app on your device, including bill photos and reminders

What is kept

Nothing is kept by us. Google may keep records of a Premium purchase in your Google account; those are managed by Google Play.

Can't open the app? Request deletion here

Open an issue at github.com/ullasankv-tech/Moneymanagerpro/issues with the title "Delete my account" and your username (do not post passwords or financial details). We delete the server copy within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes the data on your phone.
